# 10.19 charge motion energy conservation

> [!abstract] 概述
> 本笔记积累4道「带电粒子运动 × 能量」题：主线是同一条守恒恒等式——场做正功 $\Rightarrow K$ 增多少、$U$ 就减多少；所有符号都写进式子（$U=qV$、$\Delta U=q\Delta V$、$W_{\text{el}}=q\int \vec{E}\cdot\mathrm{d}\vec{l}$），不凭「吸/斥」「上/下」的语感下结论。同日姊妹篇：[[10.19 gauss interior field and E from V|10.19 gauss interior field and E from V]]、[[10.19 field maps and superposition|10.19 field maps and superposition]]。

## 1. 离子源 + 匀强场偏转：P 点末速

> [!question] 题目（截图 20261019-190911.png）
> The figure shows an apparatus that consists of an ion source on the left and a region of uniform electric field on the right. In the ion source, ions of mass $M$ and charge $+e$ are created at the far left end, accelerate from rest through a potential difference $-V_0$, and exit the source. The ions then move a short distance at a constant velocity from the source into the region of uniform electric field. In this region, the electric field is downward and has magnitude $\left|\vec{E}\right| = \dfrac{V_0}{d}$, where $d$ is the distance between the top and the bottom of the uniform field region. The ions enter this region halfway between the top and the bottom of the region, are deflected downward by the field, and reach the bottom of the region at Point P, as indicated. What is the speed of the ions when they reach Point P?
>
> - A. $\sqrt{\dfrac{2eV_0}{M}}$（我的错误选择）
> - B. $\sqrt{\dfrac{3eV_0}{M}}$
> - C. $\sqrt{\dfrac{4eV_0}{M}}$
> - D. $\sqrt{\dfrac{\sqrt{5eV_0}}{M}}$（照录，疑为排版冗余）

![[1019_ion_source_deflection.png]]

> [!info] 图注
> 原图裁剪（Figure not drawn to scale）。左箱为离子源（$\Delta V = -V_0$，虚线加速段）；右箱为匀强场区（高度 $d$、$\vec{E}$ 向下），离子从半高处入射，抛物线轨迹下坠至底缘 P 点。

> [!info] 我的答案: A — 错误 | 正确答案: **B**
> 官方点评：错在假设匀强电场不改变离子速率——A 恰是**刚出源那一点**的速率，抛物线段白送了 $\tfrac{eV_0}{2}$ 的动能没算。

### 解答

1. 源段：$\tfrac{1}{2}Mv_0^2 = eV_0$ → $v_0 = \sqrt{2eV_0/M}$（这就是选项 A 的值——出口速率）。
2. 偏转段做功：$+e$ 电荷顺场（向下）位移 $\Delta y = d/2$（半高入射、落到底缘）：
   $$W_{\text{el}} = eE\,\Delta y = e\cdot\frac{V_0}{d}\cdot\frac{d}{2} = \frac{eV_0}{2}$$
3. 全程能量守恒：$\tfrac{1}{2}Mv_P^2 = eV_0 + \dfrac{eV_0}{2} = \dfrac{3eV_0}{2}$ → $v_P = \sqrt{3eV_0/M}$，**B**。
4. 自洽检验（运动学独立核验）：$a_y = eE/M = \dfrac{eV_0}{Md}$；竖直位移 $d/2$ → $v_y^2 = 2a_y\cdot\frac{d}{2} = \dfrac{eV_0}{M}$；$v^2 = v_0^2 + v_y^2 = \dfrac{2eV_0}{M} + \dfrac{eV_0}{M} = \dfrac{3eV_0}{M}$ ✓。两条路一致：水平分量守住 $v_0$，速率照样在涨。

> [!warning] 「抛体 = 速率不变」
> 规则：恒力抛体只保水平分量不变，速率按 $v^2 = v_x^2 + v_y^2$ 涨；只要沿力方向走了位移，力就做了功 $qE\,\Delta y$。
> 防法：问「末速率」一律能量守恒横跨全程分清段，别把入场点动能直接平移到场区出口。

## 2. 三电荷系统势能：比大小 + 理由辨析

> [!question] 题目（AP Classroom Q14 · 截图 20261019-190950.png）
> System X consists of two positive point charges $+Q_1$ and $+Q_2$ fixed in place a small distance $d$ apart, as shown in the figure. System Y consists of the same two point charges and a third, negative, point charge $-Q_3$ fixed in place near the original two positive point charges. The electric potential energies of System X and System Y are $U_X$ and $U_Y$, respectively. How do $U_X$ and $U_Y$ compare to one another, and what evidence or reasoning supports the comparison?
>
> - A. $U_X > U_Y$. An external force must do negative work to move point charge $-Q_3$ from far away to its final position.
> - B. $U_X > U_Y$. The electric field due to the positive point charges must do negative work on point charge $-Q_3$ as $-Q_3$ moves from far away to its final position.（我的错误选择）
> - C. $U_Y > U_X$. The electric field due to the positive point charges must do positive work on point charge $-Q_3$ as $-Q_3$ moves from far away to its final position.
> - D. $U_Y > U_X$. The force between point charge $-Q_3$ and each positive point charge is attractive.

![[1019_system_xy_third_charge.png]]

> [!info] 图注
> 原图裁剪。System X：$+Q_1$、$+Q_2$ 相距 $d$；System Y：同一对正电荷，再在近旁固定一个 $-Q_3$。

> [!info] 我的答案: B — 错误 | 正确答案: **A**
> 比较结论撞对了，理由写反了——正源把负电荷「拉进来」是电场做**正**功，不是负功。

### 解答

1. 直接配对计算：$U_X = \dfrac{kQ_1Q_2}{d} > 0$；System Y 增加两对异号配对：
   $$U_Y = U_X + \frac{k(-Q_3)Q_1}{r_{13}} + \frac{k(-Q_3)Q_2}{r_{23}} = U_X - \underbrace{\left(\frac{kQ_1Q_3}{r_{13}} + \frac{kQ_2Q_3}{r_{23}}\right)}_{>0} < U_X$$
   即 $U_X > U_Y$，比较部分选 A、B 都对。
2. 理由辨析（本题真正的考点）：把 $-Q_3$ 从远处 quasi-statically 搬进阵中，每一步引力都顺着位移、电场做**正**功；要让它不加速冲进来，外力必须反向顶住、做**负**功（$W_{\text{ext}} = \Delta U = U_Y - U_X < 0$）。A 的表述正是这条链；B 把「吸引」想当然写成了电场做负功。
3. 自洽检验（符号链）：异号配对贡献为负 → 系统总能量降 → 场做正功、外力做负功，三方说法必须闭环 ✓。C、D 虽然「吸引/正功」半句是事实，但由它推出的是 $U_Y < U_X$，与选项给的 $U_Y > U_X$ 自相矛盾。

> [!warning] 「吸引 = 电场做负功」的功号直觉错位
> 规则：电场对试探电荷做功 $W = q\int \vec{E}\cdot \mathrm{d}\vec{l}$，号由 $q$ 与 $\vec{E}\cdot\mathrm{d}\vec{l}$ 一起定——异性相吸、靠近 = 正功。
> 防法：写 $W$ 的符号链（$q$ 的号 × 位移对场向的投影）再下结论；「把东西吸进来」天然是场在送功。

## 3. 等势线图上电子 A→B：判哪个能量增（答对 · 代表性题）

> [!question] 题目（AP Classroom Q17 · 截图 20261019-191016.png）
> The figure shows an isoline map of electric potential in a region of space where there is an electric field. An electron is initially in motion at Point A and follows the path indicated by the dashed line as it travels through Point B. Which of the following forms of energy will increase as the electron moves from Point A to Point B, and what evidence or reasoning supports the claim?
>
> - $U_{\text{e-f}}$, the electric potential energy of the electron-field system
> - $K_{\text{e}}$, the kinetic energy of the electron
>
> - A. $U_{\text{e-f}}$ increases. The electron moves from a lower to a higher electric potential.
> - B. $U_{\text{e-f}}$ increases. The electric field does positive work on the electron.
> - C. $K_{\text{e}}$ increases. The electric potential magnitude is a minimum wherever it is zero.
> - D. $K_{\text{e}}$ increases. The electron moves against a component of the electric field.

![[1019_equipotential_isolines_AB.png]]

> [!info] 图注
> 原图裁剪。等势线从左到右依次 $6\,\text{V}$、$3\,\text{V}$、$0\,\text{V}$、$-3\,\text{V}$、$-6\,\text{V}$、$-9\,\text{V}$（电势向右递减）；B 点在 $0\,\text{V}$ 线上，A 点在 $-9\,\text{V}$ 线上，虚线为电子路径。

> [!info] 我的答案: D — 正确 ✓（答对 · 代表性题）
> 电子从 $-9\,\text{V}$ 走到 $0\,\text{V}$：逆着 $\vec{E}$ 的分量移动，三个判据一路同构。

### 解答（复盘正确思路）

1. 读图定电势：$V_A = -9\,\text{V}$，$V_B = 0$——电子从低电势走向高电势。
2. $\vec{E}$ 指向低电势（指向右/右下）；电子位移大体逆 $\vec{E}$ → $\vec{E}\cdot\mathrm{d}\vec{l} < 0$。
3. 功与能链：$W_{\text{el}} = q\!\int\!\vec{E}\cdot\mathrm{d}\vec{l} = (-e)(\text{负}) > 0$ → $K_{\text{e}}$ 增；$\Delta U_{\text{e-f}} = -W_{\text{el}} < 0$ → $U_{\text{e-f}}$ 减。**D** 的结论与证据双对（A、B 把 $U$ 增减判反：$U=qV$ 里 $q<0$，去高 $V$ 处 $U$ 反而降）。
4. 逐穿越定量自洽：电子每跨一条等势线电势抬 $3\,\text{V}$，$U$ 每段降 $e\cdot 3\,\text{V} = 4.8\times10^{-19}\,\text{J}$；全程 $U_A - U_B = (-e)(-9)-(-e)(0) = 9e\,\text{J}$ 量级即 $1.4\times10^{-18}\,\text{J}$——$K$ 应增同幅。跨线计数与最终差值自洽。

> [!warning] 「负电荷从低电势到高电势 = U 增加」的符号搬用（A 选项陷阱，未中）
> 规则：$U = qV$ 必须带 $q$ 的符号——电子升电势是下势能坡；场对它做正功、$K$ 增。
> 防法：比较 $U$ 先乘号：$U_A - U_B = q\,(V_A - V_B)$，$q<0$ 自动翻号，别按正电荷直觉读图。

## 4. 3 pJ ↔ 6 pC：电势差的符号

> [!question] 题目（AP Classroom Q15 · 截图 20261019-190956.png）
> A particle with a charge of $+6$ pC moves along the $x$-axis due to the presence of an electric field. The particle is released from rest at $x = 0$. Assume that gravitational forces are negligible.
>
> From observing the particle's motion, it is determined that the electric potential energy of the particle-field system has decreased by $3$ pJ when the particle is located at $x = 1.0$ cm. What is the electric potential at $x = 1.0$ cm relative to the electric potential at $x = 0$?
>
> - A. $-2\,\text{V}$
> - B. $-0.5\,\text{V}$
> - C. $+0.5\,\text{V}$（我的错误选择）
> - D. $+2\,\text{V}$

> [!info] 示意图替代（原图无图）：符号链一张表
>
> | 环节 | 恒等式 | 代入 | 结果 |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | 系统势能变化 | $\Delta U$ | 减小 $3$ pJ | $-3\,\text{pJ}$ |
> | 势能–电势桥 | $\Delta U = q\,\Delta V$ | $q = +6\,\text{pC}$ | $\Delta V = -0.5\,\text{V}$ |
> | 动力学交叉验证 | 顺 $\vec{E}$ = 走下坡 | $+q$ 顺场运动 | $V$ 应降低 ✓ |

> [!info] 我的答案: C — 错误 | 正确答案: **B**
> 算式只保留了大小 $\tfrac{3}{6} = 0.5\,\text{V}$，把「势能减少」的负号丢了。

### 解答

1. 符号链一步到位：$\Delta U = q\,\Delta V$ → $\Delta V = \dfrac{\Delta U}{q} = \dfrac{-3\,\text{pJ}}{+6\,\text{pC}} = -0.5\,\text{V}$，**B**。
2. 动力学交叉验证：正电荷被场加速（从静止演化到 $x=1$ cm）→ 场做正功 → 粒子顺场走 → 电势沿场方向降低 → $\Delta V < 0$ ✓。
3. 干扰项诊断：$\pm 2\,\text{V}$ 来自 $6/3$ 的颠倒；$+0.5\,\text{V}$ 就是本题的丢号陷阱。

> [!warning] 「势能减少，电势打正号」
> 规则：$\Delta U = q\,\Delta V$ 两边号要一起走：正电荷系统势能降 → 电势差为负。
> 防法：模板化——先写 $\Delta U$ 的带符号值，再除 $q$（带符号），最后用「顺场降 $V$」复核号。
