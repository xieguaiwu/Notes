# 1. Module 2 Q8 — 主旨题（行进波与 spike waves）

> [!info] 我的答案: D — 错误 | 正确答案: **C**

## Task（题干与选项）

Studies of ocean wave breaking have predominantly focused on traveling waves (those propagating along the horizontal plane), so Mark McAllister et al. utilized a circular wave tank to produce and study spike waves, axisymmetric standing waves that can erupt vertically when traveling waves propagating in opposing directions intersect. Traveling waves break when wave steepness (height-to-length ratio) passes a critical threshold; breaking thus constrains wave height. McAllister et al. found that spike waves can exceed that constraint, as other factors than just steepness (e.g., jet stability and cavity shape) mediate spike-wave breaking.

Which choice best states the main idea of the text?

- **A**: McAllister et al. suggest that spike waves can form when traveling waves propagating in opposing directions intersect and that spike waves tend to be higher than traveling waves.
- **B**: The process of breaking limits the height of traveling waves, but the study by McAllister et al. suggests that spike waves can exceed those limits if their height-to-length ratio reaches a critical threshold.
- **C**: The study by McAllister et al. suggests that when traveling waves intersect in specific ways, the resulting wave may be higher than would be expected based on the properties of traveling waves.
- **D**: Previous studies have suggested that steepness mediates breaking in traveling waves, but the study by McAllister et al. shows that jet stability and cavity shape may also influence breaking in such waves.

考点：Main Idea（主旨题）

## Step 1 — Map（结构标注）

- S1 = set-up（背景 + 实验：研究集中于行进波 → 圆形水槽产生 spike waves，对向行进波交汇而成）
- S2 = contrast / 已知规律（陡度超临界 → 破碎 → 限制波高）
- S3 = claim / answer（found that…：spike waves 可突破波高限制；喷射稳定性/空腔形状等其他因素介导破碎）

文章结构：背景（研究集中于行进波）→ 实验（spike waves 如何产生）→ 已知规律（陡度限制波高）→ **发现**（spike waves 可突破限制 + 原因）。主旨题的答案必须落在"发现"上。

## Step 2 — Mark（T / F / NG 判定）

| 选项 | 判定 | 依据 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| A | F | "tend to be higher" 过度断言——原文只说 spike waves **可以**突破限制（can exceed），未说"往往更高"；且交汇机制是研究前提而非研究发现 |
| B | F | 因果倒置——原文是"因喷射稳定性、空腔形状等**其他因素介导**而突破限制"，不是"达到临界陡度就能突破"（达到临界陡度是行进波破碎的原因） |
| C | T | 完整概括核心发现：交汇产生的波可比"按行进波性质预期"的更高——对应原文 found that spike waves can exceed that constraint |
| D | F | "such waves" 紧跟 traveling waves 出现，指代 traveling waves——但喷射稳定性/空腔形状影响的是 **spike waves** 的破碎；且重心落在"先前研究 vs 新研究"的并列上，偏离核心发现 |

## Step 3 — Source-check（溯源）

- **C（T）逐元素溯源**："when traveling waves intersect in specific ways" ↔ 原文 "axisymmetric standing waves that can erupt vertically when traveling waves propagating in opposing directions intersect"；"may be higher than would be expected based on the properties of traveling waves" ↔ 原文 "found that spike waves can exceed that constraint"（that constraint = 行进波性质限定的波高）；"may be higher" 精确对应 "can exceed" 的不确定语气，同时覆盖产生方式（intersect in specific ways）与超预期结果。
- **A（F）矛盾句** = 原文 "found that spike waves can exceed that constraint"——A 把"可以突破"夸大为"往往更高"（程度夸大）；且交汇机制在原文是研究前提而非研究发现。
- **B（F）矛盾句** = 原文 "as other factors than just steepness (e.g., jet stability and cavity shape) mediate spike-wave breaking"——达到临界陡度是行进波破碎的原因，不是 spike waves 突破限制的条件，因果倒置。
- **D（F）矛盾句** = 同一句——喷射稳定性/空腔形状介导的是 **spike waves** 的破碎，D 的 "such waves" 却指代 traveling waves（指代对象张冠李戴）；且重心偏移到"先前研究 vs 新研究"的并列，偏离核心发现。

## Step 4 — Chain-check（链条验证）

文本逻辑链：背景（研究集中于行进波）→ 实验（圆形水槽产生 spike waves，对向行进波交汇而成）→ 已知规律（陡度超临界 → 破碎 → 限制波高）→ **发现**（spike waves 可突破波高限制）→ 原因（喷射稳定性/空腔形状等其他因素介导破碎）→ 主旨（交汇产生的波可比按行进波性质预期更高）。

C 落在"发现"这一环上（产生方式 + 超预期结果全覆盖）；D 是另一条链（学术史对照 + 细节词），跳过了主旨链；A（品质/程度链）、B（因果链）各自偏离主旨链。

## Trap（陷阱命名）

> [!warning] 细节词 ≠ 主旨 + 指代张冠李戴
> 规则：主旨题答案必须落在"发现句"（found that / suggests that）上，含原文细节词（jet stability, cavity shape）但重心偏离的选项是陷阱。
> 防法：回到 found that 句检验选项重心，并逐一核对选项代词（such waves / these / it）的指代对象是否与原文一致。

## Principle（对应原则）

原则四 中心问题（主旨题锁定文本中心——"发现"句）+ 原则一 文本依据（选项逐元素对原文，代词指代核对）。

# 2. Module 2 Q11 — 引用支持题（Stevens 沙画）

> [!info] 我的答案: C — 错误 | 正确答案: **A**

## Task（题干与选项）

In Diné (Navajo) culture, ikaah (sandpaintings) are created and then erased as part of sacred healing ceremonies lasting no more than a few days, but Diné hataalii (chanter; healer; cultural guide) Fred Stevens developed fixatives to preserve desacralized sandpaintings. While on a US-sponsored cultural ambassadorial trip in Europe and the Americas in the 1960s, Stevens produced several such sandpaintings and gave them to cultural institutions. This may seem in tension with the role of cultural ambassador—how could static objects authentically represent an inherently ephemeral and dynamic practice?—but such a view is itself overly object-focused and neglects how Stevens strove to convey exactly those characteristics of ikaah as a cultural practice.

Which quotation from an art historian would most directly support the claim made in the text?

- **A**: "Stevens's ambassadorial sandpaintings are best understood not as self-contained objects but as reminders of the public creations of the sandpaintings, during which Stevens conducted appropriate ikaah rituals and encouraged viewers to closely track his movements and subtle shifts in the sand throughout the process."
- **B**: "...they should not be confused with authentic ikaah, which cannot be extricated from a practice that is intentionally and necessarily transitory nor condensed into a single persistent object."
- **C**: "The most compelling way to reconcile the apparent tension... is to recognize that Stevens was an ambassador not only of Diné culture but of a US art culture that tended to value permanent works over ephemeral ones."
- **D**: "...Stevens publicly enacted the transmission of knowledge of both traditional ikaah practices and cutting-edge chemistry... a hybrid that reflects the dynamism of Diné culture."

考点：Command of Evidence — 引用支持题（Quotation Support）

## Step 1 — Map（结构标注）

- S1 = set-up（ikaah 创作后即被抹去，仪式不超过几天）
- S2 = set-up（Stevens 开发定着剂保存去神圣化沙画）
- S3 = set-up（1960s 文化大使之旅，沙画赠予文化机构）
- S4 = claim（表面张力——静态物件如何代表短暂动态的实践？——但这种物件中心观过于狭隘，neglects how Stevens strove to convey exactly those characteristics of ikaah as a cultural practice）

文本主张一句话：**"把沙画看作自我封闭的静态物件是过于以物为本的观点，因为 Stevens 恰恰致力于通过沙画传达 ikaah 作为文化实践的短暂性与动态性。"** 主张核心词：物件 → **动态实践的传达媒介**。

## Step 2 — Mark（T / F / NG 判定）

| 选项 | 判定 | 依据 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| A | T | 直接支撑主张：沙画是"公共创作过程的提醒物"（reminders of the public creations）——正是"以物件传达动态实践"的核心主张 |
| B | F | 与文本立场相反——文本认为"物件中心"观点过于狭隘，B 恰恰强化了"物件 ≠ 真实实践"的对立 |
| C | NG | 引入了原文未出现的元素（美国艺术文化重视永久作品），缺失主张核心元素"沙画如何传达 ikaah 的动态性"——解释的是张力的文化来源 |
| D | NG | 强调送礼行为与知识传递（hybrid, dynamism of Diné culture），缺失主张核心元素"沙画如何传达动态实践特征" |

## Step 3 — Source-check（溯源）

- **A（T）逐元素溯源**："not as self-contained objects but as reminders of the public creations of the sandpaintings" ↔ 主张"物件中心观狭隘、沙画是创作过程的提醒物"；"conducted appropriate ikaah rituals and encouraged viewers to closely track his movements and subtle shifts in the sand throughout the process" ↔ 主张"Stevens 努力传达 ikaah 的短暂性与动态性"。引文直接、正面地支撑主张。
- **B（F）矛盾句** = 主张句 "such a view is itself overly object-focused and neglects how Stevens strove to convey exactly those characteristics of ikaah as a cultural practice"——B 强化"物件 ≠ 真实实践"的对立，与文本立场相反。
- **C（NG）缺失元素** = 主张核心词"沙画如何传达动态实践"——C 回答的是"张力从何而来"（美国艺术文化偏爱永久作品），从头到尾未触及 Stevens 的创作过程与沙画本身的动态性。
- **D（NG）缺失元素** = 主张核心词"动态实践的传达"——D 强调送礼行为与知识传递（a hybrid that reflects the dynamism of Diné culture），偏离"沙画如何传达动态实践特征"。

## Step 4 — Chain-check（链条验证）

文本逻辑链：主张（物件中心观过于狭隘）→ Stevens 努力传达 ikaah 的短暂性与动态性 → 最佳引文 = 沙画是公共创作过程的提醒物（track his movements and subtle shifts）。

A 是链上直接一环；C 的链条是"张力 → 张力来源（美国艺术文化）"，与主张链分叉——话题重叠但未接上主张；B 与主张链方向相反（强化物件与真实实践的对立）。

## Trap（陷阱命名）

> [!warning] 话题重叠 ≠ 支撑主张
> 规则：引用支持题常有"与文本话题相关但未触及主张"的选项——解释张力来源 ≠ 支撑主张。
> 防法：用一句话写清文本主张，提炼主张核心词，逐项检查引文是否**直接呼应**核心词；与文本立场相反的选项（B 型）即使措辞正确也要排除。

## Principle（对应原则）

原则一 文本依据（引文必须逐元素对应主张核心词，不能仅话题相关）。

# 3. Module 2 Q15 — 逻辑补全（工具性论证）

> [!info] 我的答案: A — 错误 | 正确答案: **C**

## Task（题干与选项）

In the world's democracies, proportionally few elected representatives are young adults. While some philosophers argue that certain types of underrepresentation (of women, for example) are unfair in principle and should be remediated via quotas, there is broad agreement that youth underrepresentation is not inherently unfair since it is universal (all people are underrepresented when young), temporary (underrepresented young people become overrepresented older people), and reflective of a legitimate concern (lack of relevant experience). Accordingly, when philosopher Juliana Uhuru Bidadanure advocates for youth quotas for elected representatives, she does so on the basis of their instrumental value for promoting youth interests, arguing that ___

Which choice most logically completes the text?

- **A**: young adults may lack certain kinds of experience but those experiences are not relevant to the question of whether youth underrepresentation is fair in principle.
- **B**: age-related quotas would alleviate the inherently unfair overrepresentation of older people that tends to occur in the world's democracies.
- **C**: youth underrepresentation may be fair in principle but leads to worse outcomes for young adults than could be achieved with more proportionate youth representation.
- **D**: it does not matter that youth underrepresentation is universal, temporary, and reflective of a legitimate concern since it is inherently unfair.

考点：逻辑补全 — 工具性论证（Instrumental Argument）

## Step 1 — Map（结构标注）

- S1 = set-up（民主国家青年代表比例偏低）
- S2 = premise（部分哲学家的"原则不公论" vs broad agreement：青年代表不足**并非固有地不公**——普遍性 + 暂时性 + 合理关切）
- S3 = conclusion + 论证类型宣告（Accordingly → Bidadanure 仍主张青年配额，但其论证基础 = instrumental value for promoting youth interests → arguing that ___）

空格前的引导词是关键：**"on the basis of their instrumental value for promoting youth interests, arguing that ___"**。instrumental value（工具性价值）= 看**后果**。所以空格必须给出"后果更差 / 结果导向"的理由，而不是继续讨论原则本身。

## Step 2 — Mark（T / F / NG 判定）

| 选项 | 判定 | 依据 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| A | NG | 讨论"经验与原则公平"的关系——原文已用普遍性/暂时性/合理关切把原则问题讲完，空格需要的是"后果/利益"论证 |
| B | F | 与前提矛盾——原文明说青年代表不足"并非固有地不公"，B 却称老年人存在"固有的不公过度代表" |
| C | T | 正是工具性论证："虽原则上公平，但因后果更差而值得纠正"——对应 instrumental value for promoting youth interests |
| D | F | 与前提直接矛盾（"本质上不公"否定了原文的 broad agreement） |

## Step 3 — Source-check（溯源）

- **C（T）逐元素溯源**："may be fair in principle" ↔ 前提 "broad agreement that youth underrepresentation is not inherently unfair"（让步结构接受前提）；"leads to worse outcomes for young adults than could be achieved with more proportionate youth representation" ↔ 引导词 "on the basis of their instrumental value for promoting youth interests"（工具性 = 看后果）。这正是"虽然原则上不公无法成立，但从结果看仍值得配额"的论证。
- **A（NG）缺失元素** = "后果/利益"——A 只谈"经验与原则公平的关系"，是原则性论证的延伸，无任何结果导向表述，与引导词脱节。
- **B（F）矛盾句** = "there is broad agreement that youth underrepresentation is not inherently unfair"——B 称老年人存在"固有的不公过度代表"。
- **D（F）矛盾句** = 同一句——D 说 "it is inherently unfair"，直接否定原文前提。

## Step 4 — Chain-check（链条验证）

文本逻辑链：前提（青年代表不足：普遍性 + 暂时性 + 合理关切 → 并非固有地不公）→ Bidadanure 仍主张青年配额 → 论证基础 = instrumental value（看后果）→ 空格需补上"结果更差 / 青年利益受损"这一环 → C（让步结构：原则上公平 but 结果更差）。

A 回到原则链（经验与公平），跳过了工具链；B/D 直接切断前提链（固有地不公），与前提矛盾。

## Trap（陷阱命名）

> [!warning] 论证类型识别（原则论证 vs 工具论证）
> 规则：原则论证看"是否不公"，工具论证看"后果好坏"——instrumental value = 看后果，空格必须落在后果轨道上。
> 防法：定位空格前引导词（on the basis of... / arguing that...）判断论证类型；工具性论证找"后果更差/更好"类表述，并排除与前提矛盾的选项（B/D 的"固有地不公"直接否定原文）。

## Principle（对应原则）

原则二 逻辑优先（论证类型 = 逻辑轨道，引导词宣告方向，内容细节让位于逻辑结构）。

# 4. Module 2 Q17 — 标点：破折号成对闭合

> [!info] 我的答案: B — 错误 | 正确答案: **D**

## Task（题干与选项）

All member nations of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization, or NATO — including Iceland, which joined in 1949; Hungary, which joined in 1999; and Bulgaria, which joined in ___ are committed to NATO's principle of collective defense, each member pledging to defend all others.

Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?

- **A**: 2004;
- **B**: 2004,
- **C**: 2004
- **D**: 2004 —

考点：Standard English Conventions — 破折号成对闭合（Dash Pairing）

## Step 1 — Map（结构标注）

- 句子主干 = **All member nations of NATO ... are committed to NATO's principle of collective defense**（真正谓语 = are committed）
- 插入语 = **— including Iceland, which joined in 1949; Hungary, which joined in 1999; and Bulgaria, which joined in ___ —**（由第一个破折号开启）
- 内部分隔 = 分号列表（Iceland / Hungary / Bulgaria），只是列举三个国家时的内部分隔符，与闭合无关

## Step 2 — Mark（T / F / NG 判定）

| 选项 | 判定 | 依据 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| A | F | 分号是插入语**内部**列表的分隔符，不能闭合破折号插入语；结尾分号使主句断裂 |
| B | F | 逗号同样不能闭合破折号插入语——开启用破折号，闭合也必须用破折号 |
| C | F | 无任何标点，插入语未闭合，句子杂糅 |
| D | T | 第二个破折号闭合以 "— including" 开头的插入语，主句 "All member nations... are committed" 完整贯通 |

## Step 3 — Source-check（溯源）

- **D（T）规则依据**：开启标点决定闭合标点——第一个破折号（— including）必须由破折号闭合；D 的 "2004 —" 补上第二个破折号，插入语闭合，主干 "All member nations of NATO ... are committed to NATO's principle of collective defense" 完整贯通。破折号成对规则（dash—dash）是 SAT 高频考点。
- **A（F）**：分号只负责插入语内部列表，不能闭合破折号插入语；结尾分号使主句断裂。
- **B（F）**：逗号同样不能闭合破折号插入语——开启标点决定闭合标点，逗号/分号都无法闭合破折号。
- **C（F）**：无任何标点，插入语未闭合，句子杂糅。

## Step 4 — Chain-check（结构验证）

结构链：主干（All member nations of NATO … are committed to collective defense）→ 插入语由第一个破折号开启（— including Iceland…；Hungary…；and Bulgaria… 2004 —）→ 须由同种标点闭合（dash—dash）→ D 补上第二个破折号，主干贯通。

A/B/C 分别用分号/逗号/无标点，插入语未闭合，主干断裂（结构不完整）——B 的错误在于把插入语内部的分号列表当成了需要"不同层级标点"来闭合的信号，但开启标点决定闭合标点。

## Trap（陷阱命名）

> [!warning] 破折号成对 + 内部列表干扰
> 规则：开启标点决定闭合标点——破折号插入语必须 dash—dash 闭合，逗号、分号都无法闭合它。
> 防法：先删掉插入语找到句子主干（主语 + 谓语），识别插入语开启标点；插入语内部的分号/逗号只是内部分隔，不参与闭合。

## Principle（对应原则）

结构优先（语法题：破折号成对闭合 = 句子结构完整性的验证）。

> [!note] 复核提示
> 标准答案文档将此题列为"需复核"题目之一。按破折号成对闭合规则，D 无误："All member nations of NATO — including... — are committed" 是标准结构，可对照 CB 官方 dash 规则复核。

# 5. Module 2 Q24 — 逻辑过渡词（indeed 强调佐证）

> [!info] 我的答案: D — 错误 | 正确答案: **B**

## Task（题干与选项）

Upper-atmospheric jets — phenomena whereby electrical discharges cause narrow cones of blue light to briefly burst upward from the tops of thunderclouds — have been observed reaching the ionosphere. The extreme altitudes involved (the ionosphere begins about 80 km above Earth) mark these gigantic jets as outliers; ___ the majority of jets reach heights of only 20 to 50 km.

Which choice completes the text with the most logical transition?

- **A**: consequently,
- **B**: indeed,
- **C**: in addition,
- **D**: nevertheless,

考点：Transitions — 强调佐证 vs 转折

## Step 1 — Map（结构标注）

- S1 = set-up（高层大气喷流定义 + 观测到抵达电离层）
- S2 = claim（极端高度 → 巨型喷流是 outliers/异类）
- S3 = evidence（多数喷流仅 20–50 km 高——用具体数据印证"异类"判断）

前句给出判断：巨型喷流是 outliers（异类）。后句给出具体数据：多数喷流只有 20–50 km 高——**用数据印证**"异类"判断。前后方向一致，需要强调/佐证类过渡词。

## Step 2 — Mark（T / F / NG 判定）

| 选项 | 判定 | 依据 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| A | F | 因果错误——"多数喷流只有 20–50 km"不是"成为异类"的**结果**，而是佐证 |
| B | T | 强调性佐证：用具体数据（20–50 km）确认"异类"判断 |
| C | F | 并列附加——后句不是新话题，是对前句判断的支撑 |
| D | F | 转折——前后无对立，后句恰恰支持前句的"异类"判断 |

## Step 3 — Source-check（溯源）

- **B（T）逐元素溯源**："the majority of jets reach heights of only 20 to 50 km" ↔ 前句判断 "mark these gigantic jets as outliers"（数据精确印证"异类"判断）；indeed（确实）的作用 = 用后句强化前句的判断。
- **A（F）矛盾关系**：consequently 要求后句是前句的**结果**，但"多数喷流仅 20–50 km"不是"成为异类"的结果，而是佐证。
- **C（F）**：in addition 要求后句是并列的新话题，但后句是对前句判断的支撑。
- **D（F）**：nevertheless 要求后句与前句构成让步或对立，但"多数喷流只有 20–50 km"并不削弱"巨型喷流是异类"的判断——恰恰强化它。

## Step 4 — Chain-check（链条验证）

文本逻辑链：前句判断（极端高度 → 巨型喷流 = outliers）→ 后句数据（多数喷流仅 20–50 km）→ 方向一致（数据印证判断）→ indeed。

方向相反才用 nevertheless——本链方向一致，D 是跳链（把证据当成了对立面）；数据本身是中性的，要判断的是它相对前句判断的**方向**（印证 or 反驳）。

## Trap（陷阱命名）

> [!warning] 数据佐证误判为转折
> 规则：看到具体数字（20 to 50 km）不等于"对比"——后句可能是印证前句判断而非对立。
> 防法：先判断前后句方向：一致（佐证/强化）→ indeed；相反（对立/让步）→ nevertheless。

## Principle（对应原则）

原则二 逻辑优先（前后句逻辑方向判定决定过渡词，内容让位于关系判断）。

# 6. Module 2 Q27 — 笔记题（概括结论）

> [!info] 我的答案: D — 错误 | 正确答案: **B**

## Task（题干与选项）

While researching a topic, a student has taken the following notes:

- A 2022 study analyzed geotagged social media posts to assess the effect of parks on happiness in 25 US cities.
- The happiness benefit of a city's parks was the numeric difference between the calculated happiness scores of posts made in parks and posts made elsewhere.
- A benefit above 0 indicates a positive effect.
- The park happiness benefit in San Francisco, California, was greater than 0.10.
- The park happiness benefit in Washington, DC, was greater than 0 but less than 0.10.
- The average happiness benefit of parks across all cities was 0.10.

The student wants to make a generalization based on the study's findings. Which choice most effectively uses relevant information from the notes to accomplish this goal?

- **A**: Geotagged social media posts were analyzed for expressed happiness in both San Francisco and Washington.
- **B**: Though it can vary from city to city, the overall effect of parks on happiness is a positive one.
- **C**: San Francisco's parks had a positive effect on happiness, but this was not true of Washington's parks, which had a below-average effect.
- **D**: By comparing the happiness scores of posts made in parks and posts elsewhere, the study determined the effect of parks on happiness.

考点：笔记题 — 概括（Generalization）

## Step 1 — Map（结构标注）

- 笔记 1–2 = set-up / 方法（2022 研究 + 幸福收益的定义：公园内 vs 公园外发帖的分差）
- 笔记 3–6 = evidence / 数据（>0 = 正效应；旧金山 > 0.10；华盛顿 0–0.10；全城平均 0.10）
- goal = **make a generalization**（作概括）→ 答案形态 = 总体判断，不是方法复述

题目的 goal 动词是 make a generalization（作概括）。概括 = 从数据中提炼**一般性结论**：所有城市幸福收益均为正、平均为 0.10 → "公园对幸福的总体效应是正面的"。

## Step 2 — Mark（T / F / NG 判定）

| 选项 | 判定 | 依据 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| A | NG | 只复述研究方法/城市，非概括结论 |
| B | T | 基于"所有城市均为正 + 平均 0.10 > 0"的准确概括：虽有差异，总体效应为正 |
| C | F | 与笔记矛盾——华盛顿特区也是正效应（>0），只是低于平均；"not true of Washington" 错误 |
| D | NG | 复述研究方法（笔记第 1、2 条），未作任何概括 |

## Step 3 — Source-check（溯源）

- **B（T）逐元素溯源**："Though it can vary from city to city" ↔ 笔记 4–5（旧金山 > 0.10、华盛顿 0–0.10，数值不同）；"the overall effect of parks on happiness is a positive one" ↔ 笔记 3–6（所有城市均为正、平均 0.10 > 0）。让步结构（承认差异 + 总体为正）精确对应笔记数据，是唯一作出概括性判断的选项。
- **A（NG）缺失元素** = 概括判断——只复述研究方法/城市列举。
- **C（F）矛盾句** = 笔记 5 "The park happiness benefit in Washington, DC, was greater than 0"——C 称华盛顿 "not true of Washington's parks"（无正效应），与笔记数据矛盾。
- **D（NG）缺失元素** = 概括判断——复述笔记第 1、2 条方法（how the study determined the effect），没有任何概括性判断。

## Step 4 — Chain-check（链条验证）

逻辑链：数据（各城市幸福收益均为正；旧金山 > 0.10、华盛顿 0–0.10、全城平均 0.10）→ 概括环节（总体效应为正，虽有城市间差异）→ B。

D 停在方法描述（比较公园内外发帖），跳过了"概括"这一环；A 停在城市列举；C 在数据环节就与笔记矛盾（把华盛顿的正效应说成无效应）。笔记题的 goal 动词决定答题方向：generalization 要求"结论"，不是"方法复述"。

## Trap（陷阱命名）

> [!warning] 目的混淆（goal verb 错位）
> 规则：generalize ≠ describe method——复述笔记原文的选项（方法描述、城市列举）几乎永远是干扰项。
> 防法：读 goal 动词确定答案形态（概括 = 总体判断；比较 = 并列对比；解释 = 定义/身份），再排除与笔记数据矛盾的选项（C 型）。

## Principle（对应原则）

原则三 功能定位（goal 动词决定答案形态：概括 vs 方法复述）+ 原则一 文本依据（概括必须扎根笔记数据，不超范围）。

### 薄弱技能分布

### 技能分布汇总

错题技能分布（文本）：Main Idea 主旨题 ×1；Command of Evidence 引用支持 ×1；逻辑补全（工具性论证）×1；SEC 标点（破折号）×1；Transitions 过渡词 ×1；笔记题（概括）×1。

### 行动项

1. **主旨题（Q8）** — 锚定"发现句"（found that / suggests that），排除只讲背景或机制的选项；逐项检查选项中代词的指代对象（such waves 型陷阱）。

2. **引用支持题（Q11）** — 先一句话写清文本主张，提炼主张核心词；引文必须**直接呼应**核心词，"与文本话题相关但未触及主张"的选项（如解释张力来源）一律排除。

3. **逻辑补全（Q15）** — 识别空格前引导词宣告的论证类型：instrumental value → 工具性论证 → 答案必须含"后果/结果"；同时排除与前提矛盾的选项（固有地不公型）。

4. **标点成对规则（Q17）** — 开启标点决定闭合标点：破折号插入语必须 dash—dash 闭合；插入语内部的分号列表只负责内部，不参与闭合。

5. **过渡词（Q24）** — 先判断前后句方向：数据**印证**前句判断 → indeed；数据**推翻**前句判断 → nevertheless。不要把"具体数据"误认为"对比"。

6. **笔记题（Q27）** — 严格对齐 goal 动词：generalization 要"总体判断"，不要方法复述；复述笔记原文的选项几乎永远是干扰项。
